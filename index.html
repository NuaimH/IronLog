How this is calculated

BMR uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, scaled by your activity multiplier for maintenance calories. Bulk adds ~12%, Cut removes ~20%. Protein is set at 1.8–2.2g/kg bodyweight to support muscle growth, fat at 25% of total calories, and the rest fills in as carbs for training energy.
