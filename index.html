How this is calculated

BMR uses Mifflin-St Jeor by default, or the Katch-McArdle formula (more accurate) if you enter body fat %. That's scaled by your activity multiplier for maintenance calories — Bulk adds ~12%, Cut removes ~20%. Protein defaults to 2.0–2.2g/kg for muscle growth but you can override it, fat is fixed at 25% of calories, and carbs fill in the remainder for training energy.
